§ Experience

Where I've worked, what I did there, and how each role has shaped how I run a plant clinic, set up a demo plot, or talk to a farmer about soil.

§ I. Roles
01

Multi-Network Coordinator, FIPS-Africa

Farm Inputs Promotions Africa · Apr 2026 – Present

Promoted from Network Coordinator on 1 April 2026. The new role expands my scope from a single farmer network to coordinating across several at once, with a wider footprint of demonstration plots, plant clinics, and seed-system activity to plan and oversee.

  • Coordinate and supervise multiple farmer networks, aligning programmes across teams of Network Coordinators.
  • Plan and oversee training rollouts on sustainable agronomy, soil health, and climate-smart technologies at network scale.
  • Steer seed system development across networks, with a focus on clean potato seed production and distribution.
  • Standardise plant health clinic operations and quality assurance across sites.
  • Liaise with project officers, partner organisations, and KEPHIS on seed inspection and certification matters.
  • Continue field engagement: farmer field days, demonstration plots, and direct farmer advisory.
02

Network Coordinator, FIPS-Africa

Farm Inputs Promotions Africa · Nov 2023 – Mar 2026

Coordinated farmer-facing extension across multiple counties, with a focus on smallholder access to improved inputs and clean seed. The role mixed training, fieldwork, and seed system development, and was the platform on which the KEPHIS seed inspector training sat. Promoted to Multi-Network Coordinator in April 2026.

  • Trained smallholder farmers in sustainable agronomic practices, soil health, and climate-smart technologies.
  • Coordinated farmer groups and facilitated access to improved inputs and technologies.
  • Established and monitored learning demonstration plots in collaboration with communities.
  • Set up and ran plant health clinics to diagnose crop challenges and recommend solutions.
  • Conducted farmer field days and community outreach programs.
  • Supported seed system development, including potato seed production and distribution.
  • Applied knowledge from the KEPHIS seed inspection certification to promote quality seed use.
03

Volunteer, MMUST University Farm

Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology · Jan 2023 – Oct 2023

A bridge year between graduating and starting at FIPS-Africa, spent on the university farm. The role mixed practical farming with research support, and was where I first did soil sampling at scale.

  • Assisted in farm operations, including crop establishment, management, and harvesting.
  • Collected and recorded data for farm research activities.
  • Supported student practicals and on-farm demonstrations.
  • Engaged in soil sampling and testing to assess fertility and management options.
04

Intern / Attachment, Mabanga ATC

Mabanga Agricultural Training Centre, Bungoma · May 2021 – Aug 2021

First exposure to extension as a profession, working alongside extension officers. The internship is what convinced me that the long-term path lay in farmer-facing work, not just research.

  • Participated in farmer training sessions on soil fertility improvement and crop management.
  • Assisted extension officers in organising workshops for local farmers.
  • Supported soil health assessments and fertilizer recommendation exercises.
  • Helped establish demonstration plots for improved crop practices.
