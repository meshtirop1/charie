Career objective

To pursue a career as an agricultural practitioner and researcher in farming systems, with a strong focus on soil health and crop productivity. I aim to bridge the gap between research and practice by applying scientific knowledge to farmer-centred solutions that enhance seed potato and other staple-crop yields, improve soil fertility, and mitigate greenhouse gas emissions. In the long term, I aspire to contribute to Kenya's food security and agricultural transformation agenda while positioning myself as a leader in sustainable agriculture and climate change adaptation in Africa.
