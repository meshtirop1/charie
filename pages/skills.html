§ A note on proficiency

No percentage bars on this page on purpose. They've never told anyone anything useful. A 90% next to a label is a marketing claim, not an answer to "can you actually run a plant clinic on a Tuesday morning in the rain".

A more honest version: I run plant clinics, soil tests, and farmer training sessions every week as my core job. Seed potato systems and KEPHIS-style inspection are where I'm currently deepening, with formal training in progress. Microsoft Office and report writing are what I use to turn fieldwork into something a project officer or partner can read.

For a project-by-project view of where each skill has actually been used, the experience page and the résumé are the better read.
