§ I. Currently

What I'm working on

Since April 2026 I'm a Multi-Network Coordinator with Farm Inputs Promotions Africa (FIPS-Africa), after stepping up from Network Coordinator. I'm now overseeing several farmer networks at once, while still training smallholder farmers in sustainable agronomic practices, soil health, and climate-smart technologies. The day-to-day is part fieldwork, part facilitation. Setting up demonstration plots, running plant health clinics, coordinating farmer groups, and helping connect them to quality inputs and improved seed.

A growing share of the work is on seed potato systems. I sat the KEPHIS national exam for Private Seed Inspector in 2025 and am now in the official training, sponsored by my employer. The aim is to support cleaner, more reliable seed reaching farmers, particularly in potato.

I hold a BSc in Agriculture and Biotechnology from Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology (Second Class Honours, Upper Division), and I've trained as a CABI Plant Doctor. The longer goal is to keep bridging research and practice, applying scientific knowledge to solutions that work for the farmers I meet in the field.
